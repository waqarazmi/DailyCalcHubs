The 6-Phase Official Saudi Iqama Renewal Procedure

Renewing an expatriate residence permit in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia is a fully automated, paperless process executed electronically across integrated government databases. Below is the comprehensive step-by-step breakdown:

Phase 1: Pre-Renewal Verifications

Before any electronic fee can be processed, four baseline prerequisites must be confirmed:

  • Valid Travel Document: The expatriate's original passport must hold at least 6 months of remaining validity.
  • Medical Checkup (Efada): Required for newly arrived workers or specific regulated professions (healthcare, food handling, education) through certified medical centers.
  • Traffic Fine Clearance: Any unpaid violations logged against the employee or sponsor on the Murror platform will instantly freeze Jawazat operations.

Phase 2: Council of Health Insurance (CHI) Validation

Saudi Arabia strictly enforces mandatory employer-provided healthcare. The accredited health insurance company must upload the electronic policy certificate directly to the Council of Health Insurance (CHI) database, which automatically transmits verification to Jawazat.

Phase 3: Qiwa Work Permit (Karat Amal) Issuance

The employer's authorized representative logs into the Qiwa Business Portal, selects the employee's name, chooses the renewal duration (3, 6, 9, or 12 months), and generates the SADAD payment number. Once the employer pays this invoice through corporate online banking, Qiwa notifies the Ministry of Interior within minutes.

Phase 4: Settle Jawazat Base and Dependent Surcharges

Payment of government fees takes place through SADAD:

  • Jawazat Base (650 SAR): Billed under Government Payments -> Alien Control -> Renew Iqama.
  • Dependent Levy (400 SAR/month): Billed under Alien Control -> Associate Expatriate Fees.

Phase 5: Execution on Absher Business or Muqeem

Once all payments and prerequisites clear, the company administrator logs into Muqeem (for large firms) or Absher Business (for small establishments), clicks Renew Residence Permit, and confirms the transaction. The new expiration date updates across all national databases in real time.

Phase 6: Digital Resident Card Activation

The employee logs into the Absher Individuals mobile application, navigates to My Personal Information -> Digital Documents, and accesses their official digital Iqama with encrypted QR code, eliminating the need to carry physical plastic cards.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can an employee renew their own Iqama if the employer refuses?
Under Saudi Labor Law, if an employer fails to renew an employee's Iqama and work permit within 90 days of expiration, the employee is legally entitled to transfer their sponsorship (Tanazul) to a new employer through the Qiwa platform without requiring the current sponsor's consent.
