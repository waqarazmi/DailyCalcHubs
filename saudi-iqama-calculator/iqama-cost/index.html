Mandatory Health Insurance (CCHI)

The Council of Health Insurance requires private sector employers to provide comprehensive healthcare coverage for workers and their eligible family dependents:

  • Worker policy must be active before renewal
  • Co-pay maximum capped at 20% by CCHI
  • Minimum policy benefits cover emergency care

Understanding the True Cost of Residency in Saudi Arabia

Calculating the true cost of an expatriate's residency in Saudi Arabia involves more than the basic passport stamp fee. A complete financial projection must capture the interaction between government visa fees, MHRSD work permit surcharges, Council of Health Insurance (CHI) medical insurance premiums, and dependent family levies.

Employer Cost-to-Company (CTC)

Under Article 40(1) of the Saudi Labor Law, the employer must bear all costs associated with bringing the worker into the Kingdom, renewing their residency permit (Jawazat), paying their annual work permit (Maktab Amal), and providing mandatory private health insurance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can health insurance be renewed quarterly alongside 3-month Iqama?
Generally no. Most accredited health insurance providers in Saudi Arabia issue medical insurance policies on an annual 12-month basis. While Iqama and work permits can be renewed quarterly, the underlying medical insurance policy must be valid for the duration of the renewal period.
