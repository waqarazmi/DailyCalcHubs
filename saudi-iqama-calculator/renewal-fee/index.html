Understanding Saudi Iqama Renewal Fees

In Saudi Arabia, renewing a commercial residency permit (Iqama) involves two separate government entities: the General Directorate of Passports (Jawazat) under the Ministry of Interior, and the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development (MHRSD). Each entity assesses statutory fees according to distinct legal mechanisms.

Statutory Jawazat Component

The base passport authority charge covers the civil identification document and biometric immigration records. For standard corporate workers, this fee is 650 SAR annually (comprising 500 SAR base passport fee and 150 SAR human resources development surcharge). For domestic workers, the statutory base is 600 SAR.

MHRSD Work Permit Surcharge (Maktab Amal)

Commercial establishments must pay the Expatriate Financial Levy (Rasm Karat Amal) through the Qiwa platform before the Jawazat system unlocks Iqama renewal. This levy is 800 SAR per month (9,600 SAR/year) if foreign staff exceeds national employees, or 700 SAR per month (8,400 SAR/year) if the firm maintains equal or majority Saudi workforce representation.

Article 40 Protection: Under Saudi Labor Law, employers are strictly forbidden from deducting Jawazat renewal fees or Maktab Amal levies from the worker's salary.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can an individual employee pay the Iqama renewal fee directly through online banking?
While anyone with the employee's Iqama number can pay the 650 SAR Jawazat fee via SADAD (MOI Services -> Alien Control -> Renew Iqama), the Maktab Amal fee must be generated as a SADAD payment number by the employer's HR administrator through the Qiwa business portal.
How soon before expiry can an Iqama be renewed?
Under current Jawazat rules, an Iqama can be renewed up to six (6) months prior to its expiration date, provided all prerequisites (medical insurance, work permit, traffic fines cleared) are met.
