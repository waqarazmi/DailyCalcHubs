Work Permit Issuance Steps (Qiwa)
  1. Log in to the Qiwa Business Portal.
  2. Navigate to Work Permits Service.
  3. Select employee Iqama numbers and chosen duration (3, 6, 9, or 12 months).
  4. Generate the electronic SADAD billing invoice.
  5. Pay via company banking within the invoice validity window.
Industrial Sector Benefits

Under the Saudi government industrial incentive initiative, manufacturing facilities licensed by the Ministry of Industry enjoy substantial exemptions from expatriate financial levies to bolster non-oil manufacturing exports.

How Qiwa Work Permits (Karat Amal) Operate in Saudi Arabia

A Work Permit (Karat Amal - ÙƒØ±Øª Ø¹Ù…Ù„) is the official labor authorization issued by the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development through its centralized digital platform, Qiwa. Under Saudi immigration regulations, no residence permit (Iqama) can be renewed by Jawazat until a corresponding work permit has been issued and paid for on Qiwa.

Industrial and Micro-Business Rebates

While standard commercial and service enterprises pay the full 700 to 800 SAR monthly levy per expatriate worker, special exemptions exist:

  • Certified Industrial Enterprises: Eligible factories receive state subsidies covering the financial expatriate fee for qualifying Saudi-to-expat workforce ratios.
  • Micro-Enterprises: Saudi entrepreneurs operating small businesses with 5 or fewer total employees, where the owner is registered as full-time general manager in GOSI, enjoy exemptions for up to 2-4 foreign workers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can work permits be issued for 3 months on Qiwa?
Yes. Employers can issue or renew work permits for 3, 6, 9, or 12 months directly on the Qiwa platform, facilitating flexible corporate cash flow management.
How long does a Qiwa SADAD bill remain valid after generation?
Qiwa SADAD payment numbers generally remain valid for 14 days from the date of generation. If unpaid within this window, the invoice automatically cancels and must be regenerated on the portal.
