Biannual Budget Advantages
  • Fiscal Synchronization: Many enterprises align expatriate renewals with H1 and H2 financial quarters to balance working capital requirements.
  • Mid-Year Exits: Prevents sinking full-year fees into employees scheduled for planned contract conclusions mid-year.

Strategic Advantages of 6-Month Iqama Renewals

The 6-month residency renewal option strikes an optimal balance between administrative convenience and financial liquidity for private sector enterprises in Saudi Arabia. Enacted under Saudi Cabinet Decision No. 515, biannual renewals allow establishments to avoid repeating administrative filing steps every 90 days while deferring half of the annual Maktab Amal work permit levy (4,800 or 4,200 SAR per worker) to the second half of the year.

Statutory 6-Month Cost Formulation

Total 6-Month Fee = Jawazat Base Prorated (325.00 SAR) + Maktab Amal Levy (4,800 or 4,200 SAR) + Dependent Levies (2,400 SAR × Count)

How to Use This 6-Month Calculator

  1. Select Maktab Amal Quota Tier: Choose between the 4,800 SAR tier (expats > Saudis at 800 SAR/mo × 6), the 4,200 SAR tier (Saudis ≥ expats at 700 SAR/mo × 6), or the 0 SAR exempt micro-business tier.
  2. Enter Accompanying Dependents: Input the count of sponsored family members to compute the half-year dependent levy (2,400 SAR per dependent).
  3. Review Cash Outlay: Examine the itemized outputs separating the employer's statutory dues (325 SAR Jawazat base + Maktab Amal) from the employee's family dependent surcharge.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can an employee travel on exit-reentry if their Iqama was renewed for 6 months?
Yes. An exit-reentry visa can be issued for any duration up to the expiration date of the 6-month Iqama, provided all dependent fees covering that 6-month period have been settled in advance.
Does a 6-month Iqama renewal require a full 12 months of health insurance?
Yes. Cooperative health insurance policies accredited by the Council of Health Insurance (CHI) are contracted on an annual (12-month) basis. To execute a 6-month Iqama renewal, an active insurance policy valid for the entire 6-month period must be verified on the CHI database.
Can a company transition an employee from 6-month renewal back to 12-month renewal?
Yes. Each renewal cycle on Qiwa and Muqeem allows selecting 3, 6, 9, or 12 months independently, providing full flexibility to adjust renewal terms based on current corporate cash flow and project milestones.
