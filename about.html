About DailyCalcHubs

Precision calculation engines designed to demystify complex financial, administrative, spiritual, and travel decisions for residents, expatriates, and families across Saudi Arabia and the GCC.

What We Do

DailyCalcHubs (dailycalchubs.com) is an independent online computational platform developed to provide clear, reliable, and accessible calculation tools for daily life. We bridge the gap between complex official regulations, fluctuating market exchange rates, and everyday household financial planning.

Our platform brings together 7 core calculation suites tailored specifically for individuals living and working in Saudi Arabia, the broader Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC), and worldwide communities:

  • Saudi Remittance Calculator: Live SAR exchange rate comparisons and fee breakdowns for corridor transfers to India (INR), Pakistan (PKR), Bangladesh (BDT), the Philippines (PHP), Nepal (NPR), and Sri Lanka (LKR).
  • Prayer Times Calculator: High-precision Islamic prayer times synchronized with the official Umm Al-Qura astronomical standard, automated GPS geolocation, live Azan countdown, and Sunnah Ishraq & Chasht windows.
  • Saudi Salary Calculator: End-to-end wage modeling based on Saudi Labor Law (Articles 84, 85, 88), GOSI pension deductions, overtime formulas, and End of Service Award (EOSB) gratuity schedules.
  • Saudi Iqama Calculator: Itemized work permit, Maktab Amal levy, quarterly/annual Jawazat renewal fees, and Expat Dependent Levy forecasting.
  • Everyday Smart Calculator: Practical daily math tools including chronological age verification, birthday countdowns, sales markdowns, stacked voucher math, and 15% ZATCA VAT calculations.
  • Shariah SIP Calculator: Halal wealth-building simulators incorporating ethical compounding, reverse target savings, and lump-sum investment forecasting.
  • Travel & Booking Calculator: Complete vacation budgeting, flight ancillary costs, hotel stays with 15% VAT & municipal hospitality levies, booking checkout fee transparency, and group expense splitting.

Our Approach

We believe everyday calculations should be transparent, straightforward, and private. Our development approach is guided by three core principles:

Client-Side Privacy
All calculator algorithms execute entirely inside your local web browser. Your salary numbers, bank remittances, and personal details are never sent or stored on our servers.
Regulatory Alignment
Formulas strictly follow published statutory guidelines, including MHRSD Saudi Labor Law, ZATCA tax rules, and Umm Al-Qura astronomical ephemeris models.
Zero Dark Patterns
No deceptive buttons, no forced sign-ups, and no paywalls. We provide clean, fast, mobile-friendly tools that deliver instant answers without obstruction.
