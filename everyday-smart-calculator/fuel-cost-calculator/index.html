How Road Trip Fuel Cost is Calculated

Whether commuting between metropolitan business hubs, setting off on a regional family road trip, or coordinating daily carpooling, estimating gasoline costs accurately ensures balanced budgets. The fuel consumption formula calculates the liters of petrol required based on your vehicle's engine efficiency and multiplies that quantity by the prevailing pump rate per liter.

Mathematically Valid Fuel Formulas

Total Distance = One-Way Distance × (2 if Round Trip, else 1)
Fuel Volume (Liters) = (Total Distance in km Ã· 100) × Vehicle Efficiency (L/100km)
Total Fuel Cost = Fuel Volume (Liters) × Pump Price per Liter
Cost per Passenger = Total Fuel Cost Ã· Number of Diners/Passengers

Worked Calculation Example

Consider a round-trip road trip between Riyadh and Dammam (400 km each way, 800 km total) in a midsize SUV consuming 8.5 L/100km, fueled with 91 Octane petrol at 2.18 SAR/liter, shared among 4 passengers:

  • Step 1 (Total distance): 400 km × 2 = 800 km.
  • Step 2 (Calculate fuel volume): (800 Ã· 100) × 8.5 = 68 Liters of gasoline.
  • Step 3 (Calculate total fuel expense): 68 Liters × 2.18 SAR = 148.24 SAR total fuel cost.
  • Step 4 (Divide by carpool passengers): 148.24 SAR Ã· 4 people = 37.06 SAR per passenger.

Travel & Fuel Pricing Notice

Fuel consumption figures are mathematical planning estimates. Actual fuel usage depends on driving style, headwind resistance, tire pressure, AC compressor load, road gradients, and city stop-and-go traffic. Domestic retail fuel prices are officially published by Saudi Aramco and subject to periodic government review; adjust the rate per liter in the calculator to match your specific local gas station invoice.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you calculate fuel needed for a trip?
Divide the trip distance by 100 and multiply by your car's fuel consumption rate in liters per 100 km: Liters = (Distance Ã· 100) × (L/100km). Multiply liters by your local fuel price to get the total cost.
What are the official petrol and diesel prices in Saudi Arabia?
Official Saudi Aramco domestic retail fuel pricing stands at 2.18 SAR/L for 91 Octane gasoline, 2.33 SAR/L for 95 Octane, and 1.79 SAR/L for Diesel (with 98 Octane at 4.64 SAR/L and Kerosene at 1.75 SAR/L). Because pump rates are subject to periodic review by Saudi Aramco, you can customize the price field in the calculator to match any current local pump rate.
How does carpooling reduce individual travel costs?
By entering the number of passengers in your vehicle, the calculator divides the total gas cost equally, showing exactly how much each commuter should contribute to share highway expenses equitably.
