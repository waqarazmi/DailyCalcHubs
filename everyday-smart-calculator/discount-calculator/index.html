Understanding Stacked Discounts: Percentage Math Explained

During major sales campaigns, retailers frequently advertise stacking promotions, such as "50% Off Storewide PLUS an Extra 20% Off at Checkout". Many consumers assume this equals a 70% total discount. In reality, discounts compound multiplicatively rather than additively:

  • Step 1 (First Discount): A 500 SAR item discounted by 50% reduces to 250 SAR.
  • Step 2 (Stacked Coupon): The additional 20% promo voucher applies to the discounted 250 SAR, taking off 50 SAR.
  • Final Result: The final price is 200 SAR, representing a 300 SAR total savings or a 60% effective discount, not 70%.

Saudi Arabia Retail Regulations & 15% VAT Transparency

The Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority (ZATCA) of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia enforces strict consumer transparency mandates:

  • All-Inclusive Price Display: Any price shown on retail shelves, online checkout pages, or promotional banners must explicitly include the 15% VAT. Retailers cannot add unexpected VAT fees at the cash register.
  • Tax Invoices: Official receipts must itemize the pre-VAT taxable value, the 15% VAT rate, and the final inclusive total.
  • Calculating Pre-VAT Base: When a price is VAT-inclusive, the base taxable value is calculated as Price ÷ 1.15, with the remaining balance constituting the 15% VAT.

Key Shopping Seasons to Maximize Savings in Saudi Arabia

Savvy consumers plan major purchases around official Saudi shopping holidays:

  • Saudi National Day (September 23): Traditional heavy markdowns across electronics, fashion, airlines, and home goods with 20%–70% promotional rates.
  • Founding Day (February 22): Nationwide heritage sales with extensive retail and restaurant discounts.
  • White Friday / Yellow Friday (November): The Middle Eastern equivalent of Black Friday featuring massive e-commerce flash sales on Amazon KSA, Noon, and local retail giants.
  • Ramadan & Eid Seasons: Major promotions on family groceries, clothing, perfumes, and consumer goods.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does a double discount (stacked promo code) work?
Stacked discounts are multiplicative rather than additive. For example, an item discounted by 50% that receives an extra 20% promo code receives 20% off the already-discounted price, resulting in a 60% total effective discount rather than 70%.
Are advertised prices in Saudi Arabia required to include 15% VAT?
Yes. Under ZATCA regulations, all consumer retail prices displayed on shelf tags and e-commerce checkouts in the Kingdom must explicitly include the 15% Value Added Tax.
How do I find the pre-VAT price from a total inclusive price in Saudi Arabia?
To find the base pre-VAT amount, divide the total inclusive price by 1.15. The difference between the total price and this base amount equals the exact 15% VAT.
