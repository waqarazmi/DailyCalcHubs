How the Tip Calculation Formula Works

A tip or gratuity is a voluntary discretionary amount added to a commercial bill in appreciation of good service. In restaurants, salons, delivery orders, and ride-hailing services, calculating an accurate tip and splitting it evenly among dining companions ensures transparent, fair financial contributions.

Worked Calculation Example

Imagine a family dinner at a restaurant in Riyadh with a total bill of 200 SAR, 4 diners, and a voluntary tip of 10%:

  • Step 1 (Calculate Tip): 200 SAR × 0.10 = 20 SAR.
  • Step 2 (Calculate Grand Total): 200 SAR + 20 SAR = 220 SAR.

Cultural Tipping Customs: Saudi Arabia & The GCC

Tipping customs vary significantly around the world:

  • Saudi Arabia & GCC: Tipping (known as Ikramiyah) is voluntary and never legally or socially obligatory. In hospitality establishments, a 5% to 10% round-up tip is appreciated for attentive table service, food delivery drivers, or luggage staff. Many casual diners simply round up to the nearest convenient 5 or 10 SAR.
  • North America (US & Canada): Tipping of 15% to 20% is customary at full-service restaurants because waitstaff wages are legally structured around gratuities.
  • Europe & UK: Service charges (typically 10% to 12.5%) are frequently included on the final bill by law or agreement, making further tipping optional.

Voluntary Gratuity Notice

Tipping is strictly voluntary and discretionary unless explicitly specified as a formal service charge on your receipt. Check your invoice to verify whether a service fee has already been included by the establishment before adding additional voluntary gratuity.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is tipping mandatory in Saudi Arabia and the Gulf?
No. Tipping in Saudi Arabia and throughout the GCC is completely voluntary. While rounding up or leaving 5% to 10% for excellent table or delivery service is polite and appreciated, it is never compulsory.
How is the tip amount calculated?
