How Every Common Percentage Calculation Works

The term percent originates from the Latin per centum, meaning "by the hundred." It represents a mathematical fraction or ratio with a denominator of 100. Whether calculating retail sales tax, financial growth rates, academic grade scores, or recipe measurements, understanding core percentage formulas simplifies everyday decision-making.

Percentage Change vs. Percentage Difference

A frequent point of confusion is the distinction between percentage change and percentage difference:

  • Percentage Change: Applied when there is a clear chronological sequence from an old starting state to a new ending state (e.g. quarterly revenue growth, inflation increase, weight loss).
  • Percentage Difference: Applied when comparing two contemporaneous values without an implied before-or-after direction (e.g. comparing the price of gasoline in two neighboring cities).
