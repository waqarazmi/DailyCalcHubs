Frequently Asked Questions

Do you store my personal salary or bank numbers?

Never. All DailyCalcHubs calculators operate 100% locally in your web browser via client-side JavaScript. Your inputs never touch our servers or databases.

How frequently are foreign exchange rates updated?

Our Saudi Remittance Calculator synchronizes with live wholesale currency benchmarks continuously throughout trading market hours.

Can I suggest a new calculator feature?

Yes! We actively build tools requested by our community. Select "Website Feedback" or "Calculator Feedback" in the form above and describe what you would like to see.

Are End of Service calculations legally binding?

No. Our formulas adhere strictly to Saudi Labor Law Articles 84 and 85, but company bylaws and specific contract clauses can introduce nuances. Consult HR or Qiwa for certified settlements.
