Understanding the Bangladesh 2.5% Remittance Incentive

The Government of Bangladesh and Bangladesh Bank offer a statutory 2.5% special cash incentive (Pranonoda) on all inward wage remittances sent through official banking channels and Mobile Financial Services (MFS) such as bKash and Nagad.

Worked Example: Sending 1,500 SAR

Suppose an expat in Riyadh sends 1,500 SAR through Barq or STC Pay at a retail rate of 1 SAR = 32.00 BDT:

  • Base Taka Transfer: 1,500 SAR × 32.00 BDT = 48,000 BDT
  • 2.5% Government Incentive: 48,000 BDT × 2.5% = + 1,200 BDT
  • Total Received by Family: 48,000 + 1,200 = 49,200 BDT

The 1,200 BDT incentive is paid entirely by the Government of Bangladesh and credited automatically without requiring any manual claim forms.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the 2.5% cash incentive credited in Bangladesh?
The 2.5% cash incentive is automatically calculated and deposited by the receiving Bangladeshi bank or MFS provider (e.g. bKash) into the beneficiary's account along with the principal remittance.
Can I transfer money directly to bKash from Saudi Arabia?
Yes. Leading Saudi digital wallets like Barq and STC Pay offer direct bKash integration, allowing funds to reach the mobile wallet within 5 minutes.
