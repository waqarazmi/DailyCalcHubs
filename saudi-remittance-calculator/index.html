Q1: When is the best time to book cheap flights from Saudi Arabia to India?

Historical flight data indicates the optimal booking window for Gulf-to-India flights is 6 to 8 weeks prior to departure for off-peak travel, and 12 to 16 weeks in advance for peak Eid and summer holiday seasons.

Q2: How much does excess baggage cost at the airport versus pre-booking online?

Airlines typically charge between 50 to 80 SAR per extra kilogram at airport check-in counters, whereas pre-purchasing baggage bundles online costs approximately 15 to 25 SAR per kg.
