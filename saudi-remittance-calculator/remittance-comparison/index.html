Provider Detailed Overviews

1. Barq Remittance

Barq has emerged as one of the fastest growing fintech applications in Saudi Arabia. Its key selling point is its promotional zero-transfer-fee model paired with aggressive retail foreign exchange spreads for South Asian corridors (India, Pakistan, Bangladesh). Digital onboarding is performed seamlessly through Nafath authentication.

2. urpay by Al Rajhi

Powered by Al Rajhi Bank, urpay allows direct, frictionless wallet top-ups via Apple Pay, Mada, or Al Rajhi accounts. It charges an affordable 10.00 SAR (+1.50 SAR VAT) fee and provides highly reliable real-time clearing into receiving banks.

3. STC Pay (STC Bank)

The pioneer of Saudi fintech, STC Pay offers the broadest worldwide reach via its integration with Western Union. It allows users to send money directly to bank accounts, mobile wallets (like GCash, bKash, JazzCash), and over 500,000 physical cash pickup counters globally.

4. Tahweel Al Rajhi

Tahweel Al Rajhi remains the benchmark for expatriates who prefer physical branch services or sending cash pickup transfers to rural areas without extensive digital banking infrastructure.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which remittance app is best for sending money from Saudi Arabia?
Barq is currently favored for zero-fee promotions and tight exchange spreads to India and Pakistan. STC Pay offers the widest global pickup network via Western Union, while urpay provides seamless integration with Al Rajhi accounts.
Are digital remittance apps safer than bank branch counters?
Yes, all authorized remittance apps (Barq, STC Pay, urpay, Mobily Pay) are fully licensed and audited by the Saudi Central Bank (SAMA) with biometric Nafath authentication, making them just as safe as bank branches.
