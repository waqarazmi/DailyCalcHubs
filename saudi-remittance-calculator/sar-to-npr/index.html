Nepal Remittance Rules

  • Fixed Currency Peg: Nepal Rastra Bank maintains a fixed peg of 1.60 NPR per Indian Rupee, stabilizing SAR/NPR exchange volatility.
  • eSewa & IME Pay: Instant mobile wallet credits are supported across all 7 provinces of Nepal.
  • Tax Exemption: Foreign wage earnings transferred through legal banking channels are completely tax-free in Nepal.

Sending Money from Saudi Arabia to Nepal

Tens of thousands of Nepali workers in Saudi Arabia contribute significantly to their families and national economy. Due to the statutory currency peg of 1 INR = 1.60 NPR, the SAR to NPR exchange rate is directly tied to the international valuation of the Indian Rupee.

Worked Example: Sending 2,000 SAR

Assuming a live interbank rate of 1 SAR = 35.92 NPR:

  • Barq (0 fee, retail rate ~35.48 NPR): 2,000 × 35.48 = Rs 70,960.00 NPR
  • urpay (11.50 SAR fee, retail rate ~35.38 NPR): (2,000 - 11.50) × 35.38 = Rs 70,352.00 NPR
  • STC Pay (17.25 SAR fee, retail rate ~35.40 NPR): (2,000 - 17.25) × 35.40 = Rs 70,188.00 NPR

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is SAR to NPR tied to SAR to INR?
Nepal Rastra Bank maintains an official peg of 1 Indian Rupee to 1.60 Nepalese Rupees. As a result, the exchange rate of the Saudi Riyal against the Nepalese Rupee mirrors the SAR/INR exchange rate multiplied by 1.60.
Can I transfer money directly to IME Pay or eSewa?
Yes, major digital remittance providers in Saudi Arabia support instant wallet top-ups to verified eSewa and IME Pay accounts.
