OFW Remittance Essentials

  • 100% Tax Exemption: Philippine laws guarantee zero income tax on legitimate foreign earnings sent home by Overseas Filipino Workers.
  • InstaPay Instant Clearing: Up to ₱50,000 per transaction arrives instantly into BDO, BPI, Metrobank, GCash, or Maya.
  • Cash Pickup Outlets: Over 15,000 partner outlets across the Philippines, including Palawan Express and Cebuana Lhuillier.

Remittance Guide for OFWs in Saudi Arabia

Hundreds of thousands of Overseas Filipino Workers (OFWs) work in healthcare, engineering, hospitality, and construction across Riyadh, Jeddah, and the Eastern Province. Maximizing every Saudi Riyal transferred home is vital for family allowances, education, and savings.

Worked Example: Sending 2,000 SAR to the Philippines

Assuming a live interbank rate of 1 SAR = 15.65 PHP:

  • Barq (0 fee, retail rate ~15.48 PHP): 2,000 × 15.48 = ₱ 30,960.00 PHP
  • urpay (11.50 SAR fee, retail rate ~15.42 PHP): (2,000 - 11.50) × 15.42 = ₱ 30,662.00 PHP
  • STC Pay (17.25 SAR fee, retail rate ~15.44 PHP): (2,000 - 17.25) × 15.44 = ₱ 30,613.00 PHP

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I transfer money directly from Saudi Arabia to GCash or Maya?
Yes. Digital wallets like Barq, Urpay, and STC Pay support direct instant transfers to GCash and Maya mobile wallets using the recipient's registered Philippine mobile number.
Are OFW remittances subject to tax in the Philippines?
No. Under Section 32 of the National Internal Revenue Code (NIRC) of the Philippines, income earned by Overseas Filipino Workers (OFWs) abroad is 100% tax-exempt in the Philippines.
Where can family members pick up cash remittances in the Philippines?
Cash pickup is supported at over 15,000 locations nationwide, including Palawan Express, Cebuana Lhuillier, M Lhuillier, BDO, BPI, and MLhuillier branches.
