Privacy Policy

How DailyCalcHubs handles user privacy, client-side calculations, Google AdSense cookies, web analytics, and your statutory data rights.

Last Updated: September 2026

At DailyCalcHubs (accessible via https://dailycalchubs.com/), the privacy of our visitors is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy document describes the types of information collected and recorded by DailyCalcHubs and outlines how that data is used.

100% Client-Side Computing Commitment:
All financial figures, monthly salary numbers, remittance sums, dependent counts, loan amounts, and personal dates entered into our calculators run strictly inside your web browser. DailyCalcHubs does NOT transmit, collect, or store your private calculation inputs on any central server or database.

1. Information We Collect

DailyCalcHubs follows standard web hosting practices involving automated server log files. The data collected by these logs includes:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system environment
  • Internet Service Provider (ISP)
  • Date and time stamps
  • Referring and exit pages
  • Aggregate click metrics

This technical log data is not linked to any personally identifiable information (PII) and is utilized solely to analyze system traffic trends, ensure platform security, and optimize server performance.

2. How Information Is Used

We use the limited technical information collected for legitimate operational purposes, including:

  • Operating, maintaining, and improving our computational engines
  • Analyzing aggregated traffic patterns to understand tool popularity
  • Detecting and mitigating automated spam, bot abuse, and security threats
  • Responding to user-initiated queries and feedback via our contact channels

3. Cookies & Web Beacons

DailyCalcHubs and our third-party service providers use cookies to enhance your browsing experience. Cookies are small text files stored locally on your device that retain user preferences, manage session states, and provide aggregated analytics.

You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options. Detailed instructions on cookie management can be found in your browser's help documentation.

4. Google AdSense & Advertising Partners

Google is an advertising partner on DailyCalcHubs. Google uses cookies, including the DoubleClick DART cookie, to serve advertisements to visitors based upon their visits to this website and other sites on the internet.

  • Third-party advertising vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on previous visits to this or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on user visits across the web.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings or via www.aboutads.info.

5. Web Analytics (Google Analytics GA4)

We use Google Analytics (GA4) with measurement ID G-18Y3LNHDR8 to monitor aggregate web traffic, device types, and page view metrics. Google Analytics processes data in an anonymized manner to help us understand which calculators are most helpful. You may opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all websites by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

6. Third-Party APIs & External Services

Certain tools on DailyCalcHubs interface with publicly accessible APIs to retrieve real-time reference data:

  • Forex & Currency Feeds: Interbank reference feeds (e.g. open exchange rate feeds) provide indicative spot rates for currency comparisons.
  • Prayer Times Engine: Coordinates are submitted to astronomical calculation endpoints (such as Aladhan API) to compute Umm Al-Qura timings. Geolocation queries use browser-level coordinates only with explicit user permission.

Our website may also contain links to external sites (such as government portals or flight aggregators). We have no control over the content or privacy practices of external third-party sites.

7. CCPA Privacy Rights (Do Not Sell My Personal Information)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), consumers possess specific data rights:

  • The right to request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal data collected.
  • The right to request deletion of personal data collected by the business.
  • The right to opt out of the sale of personal information. DailyCalcHubs does NOT sell any user personal information.

8. GDPR Data Protection Rights

For visitors accessing our services from jurisdictions covered by General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) frameworks, you are entitled to the following rights:

  • Right to Access: You may request copies of your personal data held by us.
  • Right to Rectification: You may request corrections to any information you believe is inaccurate.
  • Right to Erasure: You may request that we erase personal data under qualifying conditions.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: You may request that we restrict processing under qualifying conditions.

Requests may be submitted to our privacy desk at support@dailycalchubs.com. We respond to verified requests within statutory timeframes.

9. Children's Privacy Protection (COPPA)

Protecting the privacy of children on the internet is especially critical. DailyCalcHubs provides general audience utility calculation tools and does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has submitted personal details through our contact form, please notify us immediately so we can remove the records.

10. Data Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard technical safeguards, including HTTPS SSL encryption across all page requests, to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, or interception. Server access logs are retained only for security analysis and rotated out periodically.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect technological changes, new platform features, or updated statutory requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

12. Contacting Us Regarding Privacy

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our data handling practices, please contact us at:

  • Email: support@dailycalchubs.com
  • Web Form: DailyCalcHubs Contact Page
