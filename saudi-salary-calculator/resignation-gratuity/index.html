Article 85 Resignation Tiers
  • Under 2 Years: 0% Payout: If you resign before completing 2 full continuous years of service, you forfeit 100% of your EOSB.
  • 2 to 5 Years: 1/3 Payout (33.33%): You receive one-third of the statutory award.
  • 5 to 10 Years: 2/3 Payout (66.67%): You receive two-thirds of the statutory award.
  • 10+ Years: 100% Full Payout: You receive 100% of your statutory End of Service gratuity.
Resignation Notice (Article 75)

To preserve your legal entitlement and prevent salary offset claims, you must serve the proper notice period under Article 75:

  • Indefinite Contract: Minimum 60 days' written notice
  • Fixed-Term Contract: Minimum 30 days' written notice or contract expiry

Understanding Resignation Gratuity Rules Under Saudi Labor Law

Voluntary resignation triggers specific legal mechanisms under Article 85 of the Saudi Labor Law. When an employee terminates their employment contract of their own accord without employer wrongdoing, the law balances the employer's investment in recruitment and onboarding against the workerâ€™s accrued tenure through a four-tiered payout matrix.

Detailed Breakdown of the 4 Resignation Tiers

  • Tier 1 â€” Less than 2 Continuous Years: The employee is not entitled to any End of Service Gratuity (0 SAR). Resigning at 1 year and 11 months means losing the entire accrued severance.
  • Tier 2 â€” Between 2 and 5 Years: The employee is legally entitled to one-third (1/3 or 33.33%) of the full award calculated under Article 84.
  • Tier 3 â€” Between 5 and 10 Years: The employee is entitled to two-thirds (2/3 or 66.67%) of the statutory award.
  • Tier 4 â€” 10 or More Years: The employee receives 100% of the full gratuity, exactly as if they had been terminated by the employer or reached retirement.
Strategic Resignation Timing:
If an employee has completed 4 years and 10 months of service, resigning immediately limits the award to 33.33%. By completing just 2 more months to surpass 5 full years, the payout entitlement automatically doubles to 66.67%!

Frequently Asked Questions

What special conditions grant 100% gratuity despite early resignation under Article 87?
Under Article 87, an employee receives 100% full gratuity regardless of service years if: (1) A female worker resigns within six months of marriage, (2) A female worker resigns within three months of giving birth, or (3) The worker leaves work due to a force majeure event beyond their control.
Does the resignation scale apply if the employer does not renew a fixed contract?
No. If a fixed-term contract naturally concludes and is not renewed, or if the employer decides not to renew, the employee is entitled to 100% full EOSB under Article 84. The tiered reduction under Article 85 only applies to premature voluntary resignation by the employee.
