Article 88 Legal Payment Deadlines
  • Termination by Employer: The employer must settle all wages, awards, and accrued entitlements within one week from the employee's last working day.
  • Resignation by Employee: If the worker voluntarily resigns, the employer must settle all entitlements within two weeks.
  • Vacation Encashment: Under Article 111, the worker is entitled to a cash payout for any accrued, unused annual leave days based on their last wage.
Final Exit Visa Requirements

For expatriate workers in Saudi Arabia, the Qiwa system and Ministry of Interior require complete financial clearance before issuing the Final Exit (Khuruj Nihai) visa.

  • Signed final settlement clearance sheet
  • Transfer of bank balance or electronic confirmation
  • Employer-provided return flight ticket (Article 40)

How Final Settlement is Formulated in Saudi Arabia

The final settlement (known colloquially as Mukhalasa Nihaiyya - مخالصة نهائية) represents the legally binding financial reconciliation between an employer and employee upon the end of their contractual relationship. Regulated under Article 88 of the Saudi Labor Law, this document details every credit and debit accumulated during the worker's tenure.

Components of the Final Settlement Statement

A standard Saudi final settlement encompasses four core pillars:

  • End of Service Benefit (EOSB): The statutory severance entitlement calculated under Article 84 (or Article 85 if resigned). Because EOSB depends on contract tenure and separation reason, you can compute your statutory gratuity using our dedicated Saudi EOSB Gratuity Calculator and enter the approved amount into the settlement form.
  • Unused Annual Vacation Leave Encashment: Under Article 111, accumulated vacation days are encashed using the statutory daily wage formula: (Days Accrued × Last Monthly Wage) ÷ 30.
  • Prorated Final Month Wages: Salary earned for the actual days worked during the month of termination.
  • Permissible Deductions: Company advances, outstanding equipment costs, or employee salary advances. Under Article 92, salary deductions for company advances cannot exceed 1/6th of monthly wage without written judicial order, but full offset is allowed against final termination proceeds with mutual clearance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who pays for the expatriate's return flight ticket upon final exit?
Under Article 40, Paragraph 1 of Saudi Labor Law, the employer is legally obligated to bear the cost of the worker's return ticket to their home country upon contract conclusion, unless the employee terminates the contract without legal cause or resigns before the end of a fixed-term contract.
Can an employer hold final settlement until Qiwa Iqama transfer is completed?
If the employee is transferring sponsorship to a new employer (Tanazul) rather than taking final exit, the final settlement must still be paid by the releasing employer within the statutory Article 88 timeframe (1 week for termination, 2 weeks for resignation) from the official last working day.
