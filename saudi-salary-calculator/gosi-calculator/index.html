GOSI Statutory Rules

  • Contributory Wage Base: GOSI strictly computes deductions from (Basic Salary + Housing Allowance). Transport and mobile allowances are excluded.
  • 45,000 SAR Statutory Cap: The maximum salary basis subject to GOSI deductions is 45,000 SAR per month.
  • Occupational Hazards: The 2% Occupational Hazards contribution is mandatory for both Saudis and expats, paid 100% by the employer.

How GOSI Deductions Work in Saudi Arabia

The General Organization for Social Insurance (GOSI) administers the social security framework in Saudi Arabia. Monthly contributions are split between the employee and the employer based on nationality and registered contractual wages.

Statutory Applicability Note: The standard 9.75% employee and 11.75% employer rates shown above apply to existing Saudi contributors enrolled under the previous Social Insurance Law. For new workforce entrants joining after July 2024 under the New Social Insurance Law (Royal Decree M/186), annuity contribution rates follow a gradual annual phase-in schedule as regulated by GOSI. Expatriate workers remain subject solely to the 2.00% Occupational Hazards contribution paid entirely by the employer.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the maximum salary cap for GOSI contributions in Saudi Arabia?
The statutory maximum wage ceiling subject to GOSI deductions in Saudi Arabia is 45,000 SAR per month (calculated on Basic Salary + Housing Allowance). Any earnings above 45,000 SAR are exempt from GOSI contributions.
What is SANED in Saudi GOSI?
SANED is Saudi Arabia's Unemployment Insurance scheme. Saudi employees contribute 0.75% of their GOSI wage base, and their employer matches with 0.75% (total 1.50%), providing financial support in the event of involuntary unemployment.
