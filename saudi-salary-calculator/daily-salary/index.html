How Daily Salary is Calculated in Saudi Arabia

Determining an employee's daily wage is a fundamental requirement in human resource operations, payroll processing, and legal dispute settlements across the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Under the executive regulations of the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development (MHRSD), knowing the exact daily salary is essential when calculating prorated pay for mid-month hires, unpaid leave deductions, unused annual vacation cash-outs, and late penalty charges.

Statutory Formula (Labor Law Default):

Unless the employment contract explicitly specifies working days (e.g. 22 or 26 days), the standard civil month is legally fixed at 30 days.

Which Divisor Should You Use: 30 Days vs Working Days?

In private sector employment contracts registered on the Qiwa platform, contracts generally classify wages into monthly payments. Saudi labor courts consistently uphold that monthly salaries cover both working days and paid weekly rest days (Fridays/Saturdays). Consequently:

  • Standard Absence Deductions: When an employee misses one day of work without authorization, the employer may deduct 1/30th of the monthly wage.
  • Unused Annual Leave Encashment: Upon resignation or contract termination, unused vacation days accumulated under Article 109 are paid out based on the employee's last wage divided by 30.
  • Pro-Rated Salary: If an employee starts employment on the 10th of a 30-day month, they are compensated for the remaining 21 days (from the 10th to 30th inclusive) at the daily rate of Monthly Salary / 30.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can an employer deduct salary for days in February using 28 days?
Generally no. Saudi Labor Law standardizes payroll on a 30-day calendar basis. Deducting 1/28th of a monthly salary creates an unfairly high daily deduction penalty. Most enterprise payroll systems in the Kingdom uniformly use 30 days across all 12 calendar months.
Is daily wage computed on Basic Salary or Total Salary?
For absence deductions and annual leave encashment, the calculation is based on the actual wage (Basic Salary + Housing Allowance + Transportation Allowance). Under Article 2 of Saudi Labor Law, "Wage" without qualification means total remuneration paid to the employee for their work.
What is the sick leave pay scale under Article 117?
Under Article 117 of Saudi Labor Law, an employee who falls ill with approved medical certificates is entitled to sick leave within a single year: full pay (100%) for the first 30 days, three-quarters pay (75%) for the next 60 days, and unpaid leave for the following 30 days.
