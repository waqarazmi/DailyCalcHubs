Key Overtime Provisions
  • Article 107 Statutory Formula: The employer must pay the worker for overtime hours an amount equivalent to the hourly wage plus 50% of their basic wage (effectively 1.5× rate).
  • Rest Days & Public Holidays: All work performed during weekly rest days (Fridays/Saturdays) or official holidays (National Day, Eid) is deemed overtime work.
  • Annual Cap: Maximum allowable overtime hours under executive labor regulations are capped at 720 hours per year, with employee consent.
Official Saudi Holidays

Work performed on any of these four official holidays entitles the employee to full overtime compensation under Article 107:

  • Saudi Founding Day: February 22 (1 Day)
  • Eid al-Fitr: 4 Days minimum paid holiday
  • Eid al-Adha: 4 Days minimum paid holiday
  • Saudi National Day: September 23 (1 Day)

Understanding Overtime Compensation Under Saudi Labor Law

Overtime regulations in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia are governed by Article 107 of the Labor Law enacted by Royal Decree and administered by the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development (MHRSD). Every non-exempt employee in the private sector who works in excess of normal working hours (8 hours per day or 48 hours per week) is legally entitled to overtime remuneration.

Exact Legal Formula (Article 107)

The statutory text of Article 107 stipulates:

"The employer shall pay the worker for overtime working hours an amount equivalent to the worker’s wage for the regular working hour plus fifty percent (50%) of their basic wage."

In practice, most HR and payroll software applications in Saudi Arabia apply a straightforward multiplier of 1.5 times the hourly wage. Where the employee’s regular hourly rate is 40 SAR, the overtime hourly rate is 60 SAR.

Weekend and Official Holiday Overtime

Under Saudi Labor Law:

  • Weekly Rest Days: Friday is the official weekly rest day for all workers. An employer may, upon notification to the competent labor office, exchange this day for some of their workers with any other day of the week. Any hours worked on that designated rest day are categorized as overtime.
  • Public Holidays: Working during official Eid or National Day holidays is strictly deemed overtime, entitling the employee to their normal holiday pay plus the 1.5× overtime hourly rate for every hour worked.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can an employer offer compensatory time off instead of paying overtime?
Under recent MHRSD regulatory amendments, employers and employees may agree in writing to grant equivalent paid time off in lieu of financial overtime compensation, provided the time-off calculation honors the 1.5× statutory premium (e.g. 1.5 hours of compensatory leave for each overtime hour worked).
Are senior managers and directors eligible for overtime pay?
Under Article 106 of the Saudi Labor Law, provisions governing working hours and overtime do not apply to chairpersons, directors, heads of departments, and persons occupying positions of high authority who enjoy executive supervisory powers over employees.
