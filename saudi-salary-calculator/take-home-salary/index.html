Key Take-Home Facts

  • Zero Income Tax: Neither Saudi citizens nor expatriate workers pay personal income tax on salaries earned in Saudi Arabia.
  • Full Gross for Expats: Because expats pay 0% GOSI pension deductions, their net take-home salary equals 100% of gross contractual pay.
  • WPS Electronic Deposit: All private sector employers must pay through accredited Saudi banks via the Mudad Wage Protection System.

How Take-Home Salary Is Calculated in Saudi Arabia

Your net take-home salary is the exact amount credited to your bank account on payday. In Saudi Arabia, calculating net salary is much simpler than in Western nations because there is zero personal income tax, state tax, or social surcharges.

Formula

Net Take-Home Salary = Gross Salary - Employee GOSI Deduction - Authorized Payroll Deductions

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is there no tax deducted from my take-home pay in Saudi Arabia?
Saudi Arabia imposes zero personal income tax on employment wages for both Saudi nationals and expatriates. Only GOSI social security applies to Saudi nationals (9.75%).
Do expatriates have any GOSI deductions from their take-home pay?
No. Expatriates have 0% employee GOSI pension deductions. The employer pays a 2% Occupational Hazards contribution directly to GOSI without deducting it from the employee's wage.
