Understanding Saudi Salary Structure & Compensation Laws

Employment contracts in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia are governed by the Saudi Labor Law, administered by the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development (MHRSD). Whether you are a newly arrived expatriate or an HR professional structuring executive contracts, understanding how gross wages, allowances, social security deductions, and statutory gratuities interact is essential.

1. The Components of a Saudi Salary Package

In Saudi corporate practice, salaries are almost universally offered as a "Total Package", which is divided into specific components on your contract and payslip:

  • Basic Wage: The fundamental core amount agreed upon in the contract. Under Saudi law, your basic wage serves as the benchmark for computing annual leave pay, sick leave allowances, and End of Service Benefits (EOSB).
  • Housing Allowance (Badal Sakn): Contractual provision to cover residential rent. In Saudi Arabia, the market standard is typically 25% of the basic salary or an annual cash equivalent of 3 months' basic salary, paid monthly or semi-annually.
  • Transportation Allowance (Badal Naql): Provision for commuting expenses, traditionally set at 10% of the basic wage or a flat monthly stipend (ranging from 500 to 1,500 SAR).
  • Other Variable Allowances: Mobile allowance, food allowance, site allowance, or shift premiums.
Why the Basic vs Allowance Ratio Matters:
Under Saudi Labor Law Article 84, severance gratuity (EOSB) is calculated on your last drawn gross wage (basic + fixed contractual allowances). However, some employers seek to keep basic wages low to minimize statutory overtime and leave encashment calculations. Ensure your employment contract clearly specifies all fixed monthly cash allowances.

2. GOSI Social Insurance Contributions Explained

The General Organization for Social Insurance (GOSI) manages the Kingdom's social security and occupational safety systems. Contributions depend strictly on the worker's nationality:

  • Saudi Citizens (Total 21.50%):
    • Employee Deduction: 9.0% for Annuity (Retirement Pension) + 0.75% for SANED (Unemployment Insurance) = 9.75% deducted from salary.
    • Employer Contribution: 9.0% Annuity + 0.75% SANED + 2.0% Occupational Hazards = 11.75% paid by company.
    • Statutory Wage Ceiling: GOSI contributions are calculated on (Basic Salary + Housing Allowance), capped at a maximum wage base of 45,000 SAR per month.
  • Expatriate Employees (Total 2.00%):
    • Employee Deduction: 0.00 SAR (0%). Expatriates do not pay any pension or social security deductions from their monthly wages.
    • Employer Contribution: 2.0% for Occupational Hazards (AkhtÄr Mihaniyyah), which covers workplace injuries, disability, and emergency medical treatment. This is paid entirely by the employer.

3. Overtime Regulations Under Article 107

Standard working hours in Saudi Arabia are 8 hours per day or 48 hours per week (reduced to 6 hours per day or 36 hours per week during the holy month of Ramadan for Muslim workers). Any hours worked beyond these limits are legally classified as overtime:

  • The employee must receive an hourly wage equal to the basic hourly rate plus 50% premium (1.5x hourly wage).
  • For hours worked on designated weekly rest days (Fridays) or official national holidays (Saudi National Day, Founding Day, Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha), all hours worked are considered overtime hours.

4. End of Service Benefit (EOSB) Severance Guidelines

Upon separation from employment, Saudi Labor Law guarantees severance compensation:

  • Contract Expiry / Termination (Article 84): Half a month's wage per year for the first 5 years, plus one full month's wage per year for every year thereafter.
  • Resignation by Worker (Article 85):
    • Tenure < 2 years: 0% entitlement.
    • Tenure 2 to 5 years: 1/3rd of the statutory gratuity.
    • Tenure 5 to 10 years: 2/3rds of the statutory gratuity.
    • Tenure 10+ years: 100% full gratuity.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do expats pay income tax or GOSI deductions in Saudi Arabia?
Expatriate workers in Saudi Arabia pay 0% personal income tax and 0% GOSI pension deductions. The full agreed gross wage (minus any authorized voluntary deductions, such as company loans or unpaid leave) is paid directly to the employee's bank account.
How is daily wage calculated for unpaid absence in Saudi Arabia?
Under Saudi labor standards, a statutory calendar month is legally standardized as 30 days regardless of whether the month has 28, 30, or 31 days. Daily wage is computed as Monthly Gross Salary ÷ 30.
What is the Wage Protection System (WPS / Mudad)?
The Wage Protection System is a mandatory regulatory framework overseen by the Ministry of Human Resources and SAMA. It requires private sector employers to disburse employee salaries via accredited financial institutions to ensure timely payment, track unauthorized deductions, and enforce contract compliance.
Does End of Service Benefit include housing and transportation allowances?
Yes. Under Saudi Labor Law, End of Service Benefit is calculated based on the employee's last drawn comprehensive wage, which includes the basic salary plus fixed contractual monthly cash allowances (such as housing and transportation).
