Why Basic Salary Matters

  • Overtime Basis: Article 107 overtime pay is legally rooted in your basic hourly rate plus 50% premium.
  • Annual Leave Encashment: Vacation pay is calculated on your contractual wage basis during approved annual leave.
  • GOSI Pension Tier: Basic salary forms the core tier of GOSI pension accumulation for Saudi citizens.

Understanding Package Splits in Saudi Arabia

When evaluating job offers in Saudi Arabia, employers frequently state compensation as a lump sum (e.g. "Total Package 12,000 SAR"). How this package is divided between Basic Salary, Housing Allowance, and Transportation Allowance significantly impacts your overtime eligibility, leave payouts, and statutory severance.

Common Market Standard: 60 / 25 / 15

Under standard Saudi corporate practice:

  • Basic Wage: 60% of gross offer.
  • Housing Allowance: 25% of gross offer (equivalent to 3 months' basic salary per year).
  • Transportation: 15% of gross offer.

Frequently Asked Questions

What percentage of a Saudi salary package is typically basic salary?
In the Saudi market, basic salary traditionally represents 60% to 70% of the total monthly package, with housing allowance taking 25% and transportation taking 10% to 15%.
Why is basic salary important for Saudi Labor Law benefits?
Basic salary is the statutory benchmark for calculating annual leave pay, sick leave salary, official holiday pay, and overtime wages under Saudi Labor Law.
