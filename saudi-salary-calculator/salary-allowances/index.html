Allowance Legal Rules

  • GOSI Subjectivity: Only housing allowance is added to basic wage for GOSI pension calculations. Transport is exempt.
  • EOSB Inclusion: End of Service severance is legally calculated on the comprehensive wage, including fixed allowances.

Structuring Allowances in Saudi Employment Contracts

In Saudi Arabia, compensation packages are divided into basic salary and contractual allowances. Understanding which allowances are subject to social security and which factor into severance payouts is essential for both talent acquisition and employee financial planning.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which allowances are included in GOSI social security in Saudi Arabia?
Under GOSI regulations, only Basic Salary and Housing Allowance are subject to social security contributions. Transportation, mobile, meal, and travel allowances are exempt from GOSI deductions.
Are fixed allowances included in End of Service Benefit (EOSB) calculations?
Yes. Saudi Labor Law Article 84 mandates that statutory severance gratuity is calculated on the employee's comprehensive wage, which encompasses both basic salary and fixed contractual allowances.
