How to Accurately Estimate Air Travel Costs

Flight booking is notorious for price fragmentation. What starts as an appealing 499 SAR promotional fare often balloons to over 900 SAR by the time you select seating, register checked luggage, and pay government departure surcharges. Our Flight Cost Calculator empowers travelers to forecast the genuine checkout total before committing card details.

1. Base Fare vs Ancillary Add-Ons

Modern airlines operate under an unbundled pricing model. Traditional flag carriers (like Saudia, Emirates, or Qatar Airways) frequently bundle 23kg–30kg of checked luggage and seat selection into their standard economy tickets. In contrast, budget carriers (such as flyadeal, flynas, or Wizz Air) strip away all services to advertise rock-bottom base fares.

  • Checked Baggage: Purchasing extra baggage online 24 hours prior to departure typically saves 50% compared to paying desk counter penalties.
  • Seat Selection: While randomized assignment at check-in is free, family groups who need contiguous seating should budget 40 to 120 SAR per sector.
  • Aviation Surcharges: Passenger facility charges, safety taxes, and terminal security fees are non-negotiable and set by international civil aviation authorities.

2. Calculating Child and Infant Airfare Rates

Airline ticketing rules distinguish between infants and young children:

  • Infants under 2 years: When traveling on an adult’s lap without a dedicated seat, infant tickets are usually calculated at 10% to 15% of the full adult fare plus basic security taxes.
  • Children aged 2 to 11: Children require their own seat and are billed between 75% and 100% of adult base fare depending on the fare class and carrier.

Frequently Asked Questions About Flight Costs

Why do flight ticket prices increase significantly at checkout?
The advertised headline fare often excludes optional or essential extras such as checked baggage, seat selection fees, in-flight meals, payment card surcharges, and airport passenger departure taxes.
How much can I save by pre-purchasing checked luggage?
Pre-booking checked baggage online during ticket purchase is typically 40% to 65% cheaper than paying excess baggage fees at the airport check-in counter.
Do infant tickets cost the same as adult flight tickets?
No. Lap infants (under 2 years) typically pay between 10% and 15% of the adult base fare plus airport taxes, whereas child fares (ages 2-11) generally range from 75% to 100% of adult base fares.
What are airport taxes and are they refundable if I cancel?
Airport taxes are governmental and civil aviation terminal fees levied per passenger. If you purchase a non-refundable ticket and do not fly, the airline is legally required in most jurisdictions to refund the unused airport passenger departure taxes upon request.
