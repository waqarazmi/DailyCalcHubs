Comprehensive Guide to the Modern Saudi Rail Network

The Kingdom of Saudi Arabia has developed one of the Middle East's most advanced passenger rail networks, anchored by the state-of-the-art Haramain High Speed Railway in the Western Region and the extensive Saudi Arabia Railways (SAR) passenger network in the Central, Northern, and Eastern provinces.

1. Haramain High Speed Railway (HHR)

The Haramain train is a 450-kilometer electrified high-speed line operating commercial passenger services at speeds up to 300 km/h (186 mph). It connects the two holy cities of Makkah and Madinah via Jeddah and King Abdullah Economic City (KAEC):

  • Makkah Station (Rusaifah): Located approximately 4 km west of the Grand Mosque, featuring modern waiting halls, taxi ranks, and public bus lines.
  • Jeddah KAIA Airport Station: An architectural landmark integrated directly into the King Abdulaziz International Airport Terminal 1, allowing Umrah pilgrims to board trains directly after baggage claim.
  • Jeddah Sulaymaniyah Station: Serves downtown Jeddah and residential districts.
  • KAEC Station (Rabigh): Connects King Abdullah Economic City and business zones.
  • Madinah Station: Located 9 km from the Prophet's Mosque, offering convenient taxi and bus connections to the Central Hotel Area.

2. SAR Intercity Rail (North & East Lines)

SAR operates extensive intercity services connecting Riyadh with key provincial hubs:

  • SAR East Train: Connects Riyadh with Al-Hofuf, Abqaiq, and Dammam in approximately 3 hours and 30 minutes, serving corporate travelers and families traveling between the capital and the Arabian Gulf coast.
  • SAR North Train: Travels from Riyadh northward to Al-Majmaah, Al-Qassim (Buraidah), Hail, Al-Jouf, and Al-Qurayyat near the Jordanian border, offering daytime passenger coaches and private sleeper compartments for overnight journeys.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I officially book Haramain High Speed Train tickets?
Haramain High Speed Railway tickets can be booked officially through the SAR Haramain website (sar.hhr.sa), the official HHR mobile app, or directly at ticket counters inside Haramain stations. DailyCalcHubs provides free route guidance and directs you directly to the official portal with zero markups.
How long does the train journey take between Makkah and Madinah?
The Haramain High Speed Train travels at 300 km/h, completing the 450-kilometer journey between Makkah and Madinah in approximately 2 hours and 20 minutes on direct services.
What is the baggage allowance on the Haramain train?
Each ticketed passenger is permitted one large suitcase with maximum dimensions of 65 x 55 x 35 cm and weight up to 25 kg, plus one piece of hand luggage (backpack or handbag). 5-liter sealed Zamzam water bottles are accommodated in accordance with station regulations.
