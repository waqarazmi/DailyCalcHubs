Understanding Prayer Times & Solar Positioning

Islamic prayer timings are fundamentally tied to astronomical solar positions relative to the horizon:

  • Fajr (Subh Sadiq): Begins at true astronomical dawn when morning twilight first appears horizontally across the eastern sky (calculated at an 18.5° solar depression angle under the Umm Al-Qura standard).
  • Sunrise (Shuruq): The moment the upper limb of the sun appears on the horizon. Performing prayer during exact sunrise is prohibited (Makruh).
  • Dhuhr: Begins immediately after the sun passes its celestial meridian (Zawal / highest point in the sky) and begins descending toward the west.
  • Asr: Standard Shafi'i, Maliki, and Hanbali jurisprudence marks Asr when an object's shadow equals its true length plus its noon shadow.
  • Maghrib: Begins precisely at sunset when the entire solar disk has dipped below the horizon, also signaling the time to break the fast (Iftar).
  • Isha: Under the official Umm Al-Qura convention of Saudi Arabia, Isha is fixed at exactly 90 minutes post-Maghrib (extended to 120 minutes during the holy month of Ramadan).
Important Observer Notice: While mathematical astronomical calculations provide reliable reference timings, local mosques may observe a 1 to 3 minute variation based on localized congregation announcements and physical visual sighting committees.

Frequently Asked Questions About Prayer Times

How does the calculator determine my location?
The calculator first detects your IP-based geolocation for instant display, then uses high-precision browser GPS (when permitted) to reverse-geocode your exact neighborhood or village.
Why do I need to allow GPS permission?
Because prayer times change as the sun crosses each degree of longitude and latitude, granting GPS permission ensures millimeter-level astronomical accuracy for your exact location.
Why can prayer times differ between locations?
Salah timings correspond directly to solar zenith, dawn twilight, and sunset horizons, which vary based on geographic latitude, longitude, and elevation.
What calculation standard does this tool follow?
Calculations strictly conform to the official Umm Al-Qura astronomical standard with Fajr twilight angle set at 18.5 degrees and Isha fixed at 90 minutes after Maghrib.
When is Ishraq and Chasht (Salat al-Duha) prayer time?
Ishraq begins 15 to 20 minutes post-sunrise. Chasht (Salat al-Duha) begins mid-morning at the dynamic midpoint between sunrise and Dhuhr, lasting until 15 minutes before Dhuhr.

Ensure device location services are enabled and click "Detect My Location", or select your city directly from our quick-selection buttons (Makkah, Madinah, Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam, Al Khobar, Dubai, Azamgarh, Lucknow, Delhi, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Bangalore).
