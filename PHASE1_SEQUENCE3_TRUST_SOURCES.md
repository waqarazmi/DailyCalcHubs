## 4. Final Factual Corrections Applied (Sequence 3)

### 4.1. AAOIFI / Shariah SIP Correction
* **Removal of Unsupported Ratios:** Removed all unverified "33%" financial screening thresholds from trust cards and body text across all 10 Shariah SIP files (`shariah-sip-calculator/` and 4 sub-tools, EN + AR).
* **Reference Methodology Labeling:** AAOIFI Shariah Standard No. 21 *(Financial Papers: Shares and Sukuk)* is cited strictly as a Shariah screening methodology/reference framework. Specific financial screening ratios are not claimed as independently VERIFIED CURRENT, but are noted as being established by each fund's supervisory Shariah board or index methodology.
* **Separation from CMA:** The Capital Market Authority (CMA) is accurately identified as regulating investment funds in Saudi Arabia (*CMA Investment Funds Regulations*), while Shariah screening benchmarks refer to standards such as AAOIFI. No implication is made that CMA sets AAOIFI ratios.
* **Explicit Non-Fatwa Disclaimer:** Every Shariah SIP trust card and FAQ section explicitly states that DailyCalcHubs provides mathematical planning tools and educational illustrations only, and does not issue fatwas, religious edicts, or official Shariah certifications, nor does it guarantee future returns.

### 4.2. Remittance VAT Terminology Correction
* **Elimination of "0% VAT" / "Zero-Rated":** Replaced all instances of "transferred principal is 0% VAT" or "zero-rated" across all 22 remittance pages (11 EN + 11 AR).
* **ZATCA Financial Services Compliance:** Updated trust cards and explanations to reflect official ZATCA VAT regulations: money transfers and dealing in money are generally **VAT-exempt** where consideration is implicit; explicit transfer fees and commissions are taxable at the standard **15% VAT** rate.
* **Bilingual Parity:** Enforced consistent terminology in English (*"money transfer / transferred principal is VAT-exempt under ZATCA financial services regulations; 15% VAT applies to transfer fees"*) and Arabic (*"تحويل الأموال والتعامل في النقد معفى من ضريبة القيمة المضافة وفق لائحة الخدمات المالية لـ ZATCA، وتخضع رسوم وعمولات التحويل الصريحة للضريبة بنسبة 15%"*).

### 4.3. Prayer Times Source & Attribution Correction
* **No Unverified Authority Claim:** `ummulqura.org.sa` is labeled as **"Umm Al-Qura Calculation Methodology / Reference"** (*"منهجية تقويم أم القرى الحسابية / مرجعية فلكية"*), NOT as a government ministry or authority.
* **Separate Identification of MOIA:** The Ministry of Islamic Affairs, Dawah and Guidance (MOIA) is separately identified as the official Saudi government religious authority overseeing mosque timetables and adhan call administration across the Kingdom.
* **No False Verification:** The card explicitly states that calculated times represent astronomical projections based on geographical coordinates and are not individually certified by MOIA. Users are directed to follow local mosque adhan calls in Saudi Arabia.
* **Logic Preserved:** Prayer calculation formulas and astronomical coordinate logic remain untouched.
