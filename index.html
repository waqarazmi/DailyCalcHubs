🏢 Saudi End-of-Service Benefit (EOSB) & Statutory Gratuity Award

End-of-Service Benefit (EOSB / Gratuity) is a mandatory statutory severance compensation mandated by the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia Labor Law (Articles 84, 85, and 87). Designed to safeguard expatriate and national workers, EOSB calculations depend strictly on the worker's last drawn basic wage, total completed tenure, contract type (fixed-term vs indefinite), and reason for contract termination.

Q1: When does a resigning employee receive 100% full gratuity in Saudi Arabia?
