Understanding Live Forex vs Retail Remittance Rates

Global foreign exchange markets operate continuously on interbank liquidity pricing. Retail mobile apps and money transfer operators (such as Barq, STC Pay, urpay, and Tahweel Al Rajhi) apply minor exchange rate spreads to cover international settlement costs. When remitting salary from the GCC to India, Pakistan, or Bangladesh, always review the exact net in-hand Rupee payout rather than relying strictly on the headline interbank spot rate.

Q1: Are NRI remittances from Saudi Arabia to India taxable?

Under Indian Income Tax Act and FEMA regulations, remittances sent by Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) from legitimate overseas tax-paid earnings directly into an NRE (Non-Resident External) or NRO bank account are 100% exempt from income tax in India.

Q2: Why does the Google rate differ by a few paise from money transfer apps?

Google displays the mid-market interbank benchmark rate. Digital remittance wallets deduct an operational spread (usually 0.8% to 1.2%) to provide instant bank account credit without branch queues.

Shariah Rules for Zakat on Gold Jewellery

Zakat is mandatory upon every Muslim whose wealth meets or exceeds the Nisab threshold for a full lunar year (Hawl). When evaluating 21K, 22K, or 18K gold jewellery, the total gross weight must be normalized to pure 24K equivalent weight before checking against the standard 87.48-gram gold Nisab threshold.
