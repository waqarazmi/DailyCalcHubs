Reverse Planning: The Smarter Way to Achieve Financial Freedom

Most people save whatever is left over at the end of the month. Goal-based financial planning flips this dynamic: you define the exact financial objective first, and mathematically deduce the required monthly investment needed to reach it.

Popular Financial Milestones for GCC Residents & Expats

  • Hajj Pilgrimage Fund: Creating an independent family Hajj fund over 3–5 years without resorting to personal loans.
  • Children's University Education: Planning tuition and living costs for higher education over a 10-to-15-year horizon.
  • Expat Repatriation & Home Construction: Building a dedicated real estate corpus in your home country (India, Pakistan, Egypt, Philippines) while working in Saudi Arabia.

The Goal Reverse Calculation Formula

To determine the periodic payment ($P$) needed to achieve a target future value ($FV$), we invert the future value annuity formula:

P = FV ÷ [ ((1 + i)^n - 1) / i × (1 + i) ]

Where:
FV = Target Goal Corpus (e.g. 500,000 SAR)
i = Periodic Monthly Rate (Annual % ÷ 12 ÷ 100)
n = Total Months (Years × 12)

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the required monthly SIP calculated from a target goal?
The calculator solves the future value annuity formula for the periodic payment (P) by dividing the target amount by the accumulated compounding factor based on your expected annual rate and number of months.
What happens if I delay starting my goal SIP by 5 years?
Because compounding works exponentially over time, delaying your start reduces the time your profits have to generate further returns, frequently doubling the required monthly deposit required to hit the same target.
Can I use this goal calculator for a Hajj or Umrah fund?
Yes. Simply input your estimated total pilgrimage package cost and desired timeline to find the disciplined monthly amount to set aside in Shariah-compliant short-to-medium term Sukuk or cash funds.
